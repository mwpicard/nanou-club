S/V NANOU · Bruce Roberts design · steel

The boat

Built 1986 in Durban, South Africa. 16.5 m, 35 tons of marine-grade steel, cutter rigged, flying the Australian flag. Range: 1,800 nautical miles between fuel stops — and she has crossed every ocean she's met.

Accommodation

  • Master cabin with ensuite — 1 double berth
  • Guest cabin — 1 double berth
  • 4 single berths and a couch berth in the saloon
  • Forward crew cabin with second head — reserved for the paid crew

Hot bunking is a Nanou tradition — on passage, more souls than berths is normal. Berth counts on the chart are guidance, not walls.

Specifications

Sailing

  • Cutter rig, 22 m mast
  • Two head sails, storm staysail, mainsail
  • Twin genoas with double whisker poles

Machinery

  • Perkins M130 diesel, 135 HP (1997)
  • 600 W solar, wind generator, 6 kVA silent diesel
  • Electric windlass, 150 m of heavy chain

Water & comfort

  • 4,800 L drinking water + 100 L/h watermaker
  • Washer/dryer, fridge and separate freezer
  • Boarding platform, 3.5 m tender with 10 HP outboard

Adventure

  • Poseidon dive compressor, equipment for 4 divers
  • 10-person liferaft with watermaker and EPIRB
  • VHF, HF radio, satellite email

Recommissioning

She needs several weeks of yard work, a new mainsail and some electrics; a bow thruster is under study to make her friendlier in tight harbours. Progress updates will be posted here by the crew as the work happens.
